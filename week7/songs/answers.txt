A way of calculating the audio aura is the following:
SELECT AVG(energy) * AVG(danceability) * AVG(valence) FROM songs;
Since all the metrics range from 0 to 1, their product tends to be low unless all three averages are close to 1.
Therefore, it is only measuring how euphoric the songs are on average rather than measuring mood in general.
Calm but cheerful music, for example, get a low score, just like sad music. Taking the cube root of the product
(the geometric mean) doesn't change how different auras compare to each other, but it brings the result back to the original scale of 0 to 1,
which makes it easier to interpret. Another way of representing this aura can be by defining differnt thresholds for the average
values, each representing different states.

SELECT
    CASE
        WHEN AVG(energy) >= 0.5 AND AVG(valence) >= 0.5 THEN 'Energetic and cheerful'
        WHEN AVG(energy) >= 0.5                         THEN 'Intense'
        WHEN AVG(valence) >= 0.5                        THEN 'Chill'
        ELSE                                                 'Melancholic'
    END AS aura
FROM songs;

One should notice that the 0.5 threshold is arbitrary.
On top of the averages, the standard deviation should also be included, as the average tends to be a biased metric depending on the set it is taken from.
In this case, it does not count for the variability of the different songs; a mix of energetic and sad songs would yield the same average as a mix of neutral ones.
It also treats all the songs equaly, when not all of them are streamed an equal amount of time. Therefore, theaverage should actually be taken as a weighted
average by number of streams.
